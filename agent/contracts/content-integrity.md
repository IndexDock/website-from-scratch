# Content integrity contract

- Never invent reviews, awards, credentials, years in business, guarantees, customers, response times, statistics, or evidence.
- Mark unknown business facts for learner confirmation instead of converting them into copy.
- Important facts must appear in rendered text, not only metadata, schema, images, sliders, or hidden interactions.
- Every page has one primary purpose and one primary call to action.
- Give each distinct visitor need its own page when there is enough verified content to support it, rather than collapsing every need into homepage sections by default. A single page is valid only for a genuinely minimal business with one visitor need and not enough distinct content to justify more.
- Never render a contact form without a real approved endpoint.
- Ask the learner to upload available logos and photographs on GitHub, as `agent/contracts/assets.md` describes, and place the files yourself. Never treat a missing asset as permission to invent factual imagery.
- Question headings, direct answers, FAQs, metadata, and structured data must remain grounded in approved visible facts. Gather FAQ questions and answers directly from the learner during the business plan; never invent them to fill a section, and skip FAQ entirely when the learner has none to share.
