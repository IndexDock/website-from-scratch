# Scope contract

Starter supports one language, one to five core public pages, and a simple informational or lead-generation website. Direct contact actions and verified external services are allowed.

Reject or reframe ecommerce checkout, accounts, dashboards, marketplaces, databases, subscriptions, custom booking systems, sensitive stored data, large CMS or blog systems, multilingual corporate sites, and custom web applications.

Presentation and enquiries belong inside Starter. Transactions, accounts, stored workflows, and business operations do not.

The required interview identifies the learner's occupation or business, the website outcome, the intended visitors, and what those visitors must be able to do. Keep those details concise enough to preserve the two-question entry experience.
